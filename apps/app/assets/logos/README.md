Logos of the connections, drawn in one color.

- claude.svg, openai.svg, ollama.svg: from Simple Icons 15.10.0 (CC0).
- cursor.svg, grok.svg, antigravity.svg, opencode.svg: the icons the ACP registry lists for
  them (cdn.agentclientprotocol.com/registry/v1/latest, 2026-11-12), bundled because they
  are first-party connections in Helmy.

The marks themselves belong to Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama, Anysphere, xAI, Google and
Anomaly. Other agents from the ACP registry use the icon the registry lists.
