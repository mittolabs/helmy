---
title: What Is Helmy?
description: One assistant for every AI you pay for, on macOS, Windows, Linux and your phone.
---

Helmy is an assistant that does the work you ask for: in your folders, in your accounts, in its Browser panel, or on a Design canvas beside the chat. It works through the AI subscriptions you already have, such as Claude and ChatGPT, plus a free connection that needs no account, so you can stop switching between AI apps and keep one conversation going.

Why it matters:

- **No more switching apps.** Helmy picks the right connection for each message, and when one runs out, it moves the conversation to another, history and all.
- **No model picker.** Say what you want done. Helmy decides which connection answers, and how.
- **Fewer interruptions.** Helmy checks each action against what you asked for, and only asks you about what can't be undone.
- **Keys out of the chat.** The AI uses your API keys by name and never sees them.
- **Your chats in your pocket.** Pair your phone, and follow, approve and stop work from anywhere.
