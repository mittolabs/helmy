Helmy is an assistant for your computer, on macOS, Windows and Linux, with an app for your phone. You tell it what you want done, and it does the work: in your folders, in your accounts, on a page in its browser, or on a design canvas beside the chat.

You don't need to choose an AI to use it. Helmy works through the AI subscriptions you already have, such as Claude and ChatGPT, and through a free connection that needs no account. For each message, it picks the one that can do what you asked and has the most room left. When one runs out, Helmy moves the conversation to another, and you keep going.

## What makes it different

- **It chooses for you.** There's no model picker and no effort dial. Helmy decides which connection answers each message. Learn more in [Connections](/connections).
- **It asks only what matters.** Helmy checks each action against what you asked for, and only asks you about what can't be undone. Learn more in [Approvals](/approvals).
- **Your keys stay out of the chat.** The AI uses your API keys by name and never sees them. Learn more in [The vault](/vault).
- **Everything happens beside the chat.** A browser, a terminal, files, changes, devices and a design canvas open next to the conversation. Learn more in [Beside the chat](/beside-the-chat).
- **Your phone comes along.** Pair it once, and your chats follow you. Learn more in [Your phone](/phone).
