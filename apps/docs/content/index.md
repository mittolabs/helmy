title: What Helmy is
description: An assistant on your Mac that gets things done in your folders and accounts.
---

Helmy is an assistant on your Mac. You tell it what you want done, and it does it: in your folders, in your accounts, on a page in its browser, on a phone simulator, or on a design canvas beside the chat.

It does the work through AI tools you already have: **Claude Code**, **Codex**, **OpenCode** and, on this Mac, **Ollama**. Helmy calls them *connections*. You add them once, and after that you only talk to Helmy: for each message it picks the connection that can do what you asked and has the most of its usage limit left.

## What makes it different

- **It chooses for you.** No model pickers, no effort dials. Helmy decides which connection answers, and moves a thread to another when one runs low.
- **It asks only what matters.** Agents' actions are judged by Helmy against what you asked for. You are asked only about what can't be undone or leaves your Mac. See [Approvals](/approvals).
- **Secrets stay out of the AI's sight.** API keys live in a vault in your Keychain; agents use them by name. See [The vault](/vault).
- **Everything beside the chat.** A browser, a terminal, files, changes, a device, a design canvas, each in a tab next to the conversation, or in a window of its own.
- **Your phone comes along.** Pair it once and your chats follow you. See [Your phone](/phone).
